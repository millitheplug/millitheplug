## `whoami`

I'm a privacy and information security professional building deeper expertise at the intersection of **data protection, security governance, risk, and AI governance**.

My approach is practical: understand the risk, make controls understandable, protect people and data, and help teams use technology responsibly.

- **Data privacy:** privacy principles, data handling, accountability, and privacy-by-design thinking
- **Information security:** risk-aware security practices, policies, controls, and governance
- **AI governance:** responsible AI, AI risk management, transparency, and oversight
- **GRC:** connecting requirements and frameworks to clear, actionable practices

> Current direction: bringing privacy, security, and AI governance together instead of treating them as separate checklists.

## `working_principles`

- Privacy and security should be considered from the start.
- Governance should be clear enough to use—not just impressive on paper.
- Risk decisions should be documented, proportionate, and reviewable.
- AI systems need accountability, human oversight, and ongoing evaluation.
- Good controls should protect people while helping teams do their work.
